Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Florida Claim Review is a marketing website for property inspection and public adjusting services associated with Caliber Claim Adjusters. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit floridaclaimreview.com, request an inspection or consultation, contact us, or submit information through our Facebook or Instagram lead forms.

1. Information we collect

Depending on what you submit or discuss with us, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Your property address, property damage concerns, and inspection requests.
  • Insurance claim details you choose to provide, such as the type of damage, claim status, and related information needed to respond to your inquiry.
  • Your messages, preferred language, preferred contact method, and other information you voluntarily provide.
  • Technical and interaction information collected by our website and providers, such as IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, and interactions with inspection and contact features.

Please provide only information relevant to your request. Do not send Social Security numbers, passwords, bank account details, or other unnecessary sensitive information through our general inquiry forms.

2. How we collect information

We collect information directly from you through website inspection forms, the Quick Review feature, phone calls, emails, and follow-up communications. Website form submissions are processed by Formspree and delivered by email to our business Gmail inbox.

When you submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form associated with Florida Claim Review, Meta makes the information you submit available to us. This may include contact information prefilled from your Meta profile that you choose to submit, answers to our questions, property or claim information you provide, and your inspection or consultation request. Meta also handles information under its own Privacy Policy.

3. How we use information

We use information to contact homeowners about their inquiries, schedule free property inspections, provide property damage and insurance claim consultations, coordinate requested services with Caliber Claim Adjusters, respond to service requests, and maintain related communications. We also use technical and interaction information to operate the website, understand its performance, and measure advertising and form conversions.

Information submitted through Meta lead forms is used to follow up on the request described in the form and provide the inspection or consultation information you requested. You may ask us to stop follow-up marketing communications by contacting us using the details below.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information or leads.

We share homeowner leads only with Caliber Claim Adjusters and service providers that support our website, communications, and requested services. We share information with Caliber Claim Adjusters as needed to respond to your request and coordinate property inspection or public adjusting services. Service providers also process information to support the website and communications, including Formspree for form processing, Google/Gmail and email-delivery providers for email, and Vercel for website hosting. We do not use a customer relationship management (CRM) system.

We do not upload customer lists to Meta. Meta receives website interaction information through the Meta Pixel as described below. This advertising-related disclosure is separate from selling homeowner information or leads. Privacy laws may define advertising-related “sharing” or “sale” differently from ordinary commercial sales; our statement about not selling information does not mean no information is disclosed to advertising providers.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with applicable law or a valid legal request, protect rights and safety, or carry out a disclosure you request or authorize. Third-party services have their own privacy and security practices.

5. Storage and protection

Website submissions are transmitted over HTTPS to Formspree and delivered to our business email account. Information is stored in the form-processing and email services used to handle inquiries, and may be included in communications and service records associated with your request. Within Florida Claim Review, only Joshua Londono and Gianclaudio Capossela have access to homeowner leads. Caliber Claim Adjusters and service providers may process information as described in the sharing section above. These providers use their own hosting, account, and security systems.

HTTPS helps protect information in transit. The security of stored information also depends on provider safeguards, account security, and how records are handled. No website, transmission method, or storage system can guarantee complete security.

Retention depends on the purpose of the inquiry, ongoing service needs, provider settings, and applicable recordkeeping requirements. This policy does not establish a fixed retention period. You may contact us to ask about information held about you or request deletion, subject to applicable legal and service-related requirements.

6. Cookies, analytics, and advertising technologies

The homepage uses the Meta Pixel to record page views and interactions, including contact actions and successful lead submissions. Meta may use cookies or similar technologies and receive information such as your IP address, browser/device information, page URL, and interaction events to measure ads, understand conversions, and support advertising personalization under its policies. Meta may associate this information with your Facebook or Instagram account.

The website also uses browser local storage to remember your English or Spanish language preference. Hosting and form-processing providers may collect technical logs needed to operate and protect their services. This Privacy Policy page itself does not load the Meta Pixel.

You can manage cookies in your browser and advertising preferences in Meta's Ad Preferences. Blocking cookies or tracking technologies may affect some website or advertising features. Browser cookie controls may not remove local storage or stop every type of data collection.

7. Access, correction, deletion, and contact choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by emailing flclaimreview@gmail.com or calling a contact below. Tell us how to identify your request and what you would like us to do. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify your identity and locate the relevant records; please do not send unnecessary sensitive documents.

We will review requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for ongoing services, legal obligations, or other permitted purposes. You can also ask us to stop marketing follow-up or change your preferred contact method. For information held independently by Meta or another provider, you may also need to use that provider's privacy controls.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy as our services, technologies, or data practices change. Updated policies will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date. Please review this page periodically. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent, those requirements apply.
